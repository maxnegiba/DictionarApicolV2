# Editorial policy / Politica editorială

## Two independent editions
- RO: *Dicționar Apicol — Enciclopedie și Manual Practic*.
- EN: *The Beekeeping Dictionary — Encyclopedia & Practical Handbook* (working title).
- Shared concept IDs and evidence register; **separately written and reviewed** RO/EN prose.
- RO regulatory default: Romania and EU. EN scope: international; every location-dependent claim names its jurisdiction.
- Neither edition inherits an approval or a translation automatically from the other.

## Content classes
TER dictionary entry; CON explanatory chapter; SOP standard operating procedure; TAX botanical profile; PAT disease/health profile; REG law/regulation; DAT table or dataset; IMG visual asset.

## Workflow
DRAFT -> RESEARCHED -> FACT_CHECKED -> LANGUAGE_REVIEWED -> APPROVED.
BLOCKED or REJECTED are separate dispositions. Text may not claim scientific validation on the strength of AI-generated review alone.

## Mandatory evidence gates
Every critical scientific claim needs a traceable source, pinpoint or URL, access date, and notes about what the source actually supports.
Historic apicultural treatments are evidence for history, **not** instructions to use.
Veterinary procedures and high-risk field operations require documented qualified external review and applicable authorization before they can be approved for publication.
Translation is not a substitute for native terminology research or locale-specific legal checks.
An automated schema check does **not** mean a claim is scientifically true.

## Public repository rule
Do not import manuscript V1, full audit CSV, original Word drafts, confidential notes, licensed articles, or private reviewers' details while repository visibility is public. Store source hashes and metadata only until approved private storage is available.
